529. B. The correct answer is B (52°): complementary angles add up to 90°, so the complement is 90° − 38° = 52°. The measure 142° is the supplement, found by subtracting from 180° instead of 90°. The measure 322° comes from subtracting 38° from 360°, which is the total for a full turn. The measure 128° comes from adding 38° to 90° instead of subtracting it.

530. C. The correct answer is C (108°): supplementary angles add up to 180°, so the supplement is 180° − 72° = 108°. The measure 18° is the complement, found by subtracting from 90° instead of 180°. The measure 288° comes from subtracting 72° from 360°. The measure 162° comes from adding 72° to 90°.

531. C. The correct answer is C (65°): the two known angles add to 48° + 67° = 115°, and 180° − 115° = 65°. The measure 115° is the sum of the two known angles, not the missing one. The measure 245° comes from subtracting 115° from 360°, which is the total for a quadrilateral. The measure 75° comes from adding 48° and 67° as 105° instead of 115°.

532. B. The correct answer is B (62°): the angles add to 180°, so the third angle is 180° − 90° − 28° = 62°. The measure 152° subtracts only the 28° angle and leaves out the right angle. The measure 118° comes from adding 28° to the right angle. The measure 242° uses 360° as the total, which is the rule for quadrilaterals.

533. D. The correct answer is D (85°): the three known angles add to 90° + 110° + 75° = 275°, and 360° − 275° = 85°. The measure 275° is the sum of the three known angles, not the missing one. The measure 185° comes from subtracting only two of the known angles from 360°. The measure 105° comes from adding the three known angles as 255° instead of 275°.

534. C. The correct answer is C (Isosceles): two of the sides are equal, so the triangle is isosceles. An equilateral triangle needs three equal sides, but one side here is 10 inches. A scalene triangle has no equal sides, but two sides here are 7 inches. Nothing shows a 90° angle, and the sides do not form a right triangle.

535. C. The correct answer is C (Obtuse): 135° is more than 90° but less than 180°, so it is obtuse. An acute angle is less than 90°, and 135° is greater. A right angle is exactly 90°. A straight angle is exactly 180°, and 135° is less.

536. A. The correct answer is A (8): an octagon has 8 sides. A shape with 6 sides is a hexagon. A shape with 5 sides is a pentagon. A shape with 10 sides is not an octagon, which has 8.

537. A. The correct answer is A (A square is a special kind of rectangle): a square has four right angles and opposite sides that are equal, so it meets the definition of a rectangle. A rectangle does not need four equal sides, so it is not a special kind of square. A rhombus has four equal sides, but its angles do not need to be right angles. A parallelogram has two pairs of parallel sides, not one.

538. D. The correct answer is D (They meet to form right angles): perpendicular lines meet to form a square corner, which is a 90° right angle. Lines that stay the same distance apart are parallel, not perpendicular. An obtuse angle is more than 90°, so it is not a square corner. Length has nothing to do with whether lines are perpendicular, and lines go on without end.

The measure 135° is the larger angle, not the smaller one.
