Two counts only the punches and forgets that each punch goes through two layers. Three is not possible, because each punch makes the same number of holes. Eight would need four layers, which takes two folds.

982. B. The correct answer is B (6): only the centre cube of each face has exactly one painted face, and a cube has 6 faces. Twelve is the number of edge cubes, which have paint on two faces. Eight is the number of corner cubes, which have paint on three faces. Nine counts every small cube on one face of the large cube, not just the centre cube.

983. B. The correct answer is B (4): a square has 2 lines through opposite corners and 2 lines through the middles of opposite sides, which makes 4. Two counts only one kind of line and leaves out the others. Eight counts each line twice, once for each end of the line. One counts only a single line instead of each line that splits the square into mirror halves.

984. A. The correct answer is A (the square of four boxes with 4 boxes shaded): the boxes are shaded clockwise starting at the top left, so the fourth figure has the last box, the bottom left, shaded too. The square of four boxes with 2 boxes shaded goes back to an earlier step instead of adding a box. The square of four boxes with only the top left box shaded returns to the first step of the pattern. The square of four boxes with no boxes shaded removes the shading instead of adding a box.

985. D. The correct answer is D (the circle with its hand pointing right): the hand turns a quarter turn counterclockwise at each step, from up to left to down, so it points right next. The circle with its hand pointing left repeats the second figure instead of continuing the turn. The circle with its hand pointing up returns the hand to its start one step too early. The circle with its hand pointing down and to the right turns the hand only part of a quarter turn.

986. D. The correct answer is D (a large white hexagon with 6 lines): the triangle with 3 lines becomes a square with 4 lines, so the pentagon with 5 lines becomes a hexagon with 6 lines. A large white hexagon with 5 lines adds a side but keeps the same number of lines. A large white pentagon with 6 lines adds a line but keeps the same shape. A large white square with 4 lines copies the second figure instead of changing the pentagon.

987. B. The correct answer is B (a black arrow pointing left): the arrow pointing up makes a half turn and turns black, so the arrow pointing right becomes a black arrow pointing left. A white arrow pointing left turns correctly but leaves out the change to black. A black arrow pointing right turns black but does not turn around. A black arrow pointing down copies the second figure instead of changing the third arrow.

988. C. The correct answer is C (a white pentagon with 2 lines and 3 dots below it): each of the other figures has as many dots as lines, but the pentagon has 2 lines and 3 dots. A white circle with 3 lines and 3 dots below it has the same number of lines and dots, like the others. A white square with 2 lines and 2 dots below it has the same number of lines and dots, like the others. A white hexagon with 4 lines and 4 dots below it has the same number of lines and dots, like the others.

989. A. A small black square is the right shape, but the middle row already has a black figure. A small white circle is the right shading, but the middle row already has a circle. A small grey triangle repeats a shape and a shading that the middle row already has.

990. C. The correct answer is C (a large white square with 6 dots): in each row the third box has the first count times the second count, so 3 × 2 gives 6 dots. A large white square with 5 dots adds the first two boxes, but adding does not work for the first row, where 2 + 1 is not 2. A large white square with 3 dots copies the first box of the row instead of using the rule. A large white square with 4 dots repeats the third box of the row above instead of using the rule.

991. C. The correct answer is C (the box with a dot in the middle of the left edge): the dot moves 2 stops clockwise each time, so from the middle of the bottom edge it moves to the middle of the left edge. 992. D. The correct answer is D (The pool was not open when Marisol rode past it): the result did not happen, because no lifeguard was in the chair, so by the contrapositive the pool was not open. An open pool without a lifeguard in the chair would break the statement, which is given as true. The facts say nothing about what happens later in the day. The facts never mention a sick lifeguard, so this adds a reason that was not given.

993. B. 994. A. The correct answer is A (Jake): Hugo is first, Fiona and Gia must be second and third, and Kai must be fourth, which leaves Jake last. Kai did not finish last, according to the clues. Gia finished third, directly behind Fiona. Fiona finished second, right after Hugo.

995. B. The correct answer is B (sprint): a shout is a much stronger form of a whisper, and a sprint is a much stronger form of a walk. Stroll is a gentler form of walk, not a stronger one. 998. C. 1000. D. The correct answer is D (58): row 1 has 10 seats and 2 seats are added 24 times to reach row 25, so 10 + 2 × 24 = 58. Sixty adds 2 seats 25 times, one time too many.
